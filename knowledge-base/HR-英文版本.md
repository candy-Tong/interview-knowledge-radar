5. 你认为自己有什么需要改进的地方？ / What is one area you want to improve?

我以前遇到工程问题时，容易希望一次把方案做得很完整。但在大仓治理中，我逐步认识到，技术上正确不代表当前就适合推动。现在我会先看业务价值、改造成本、风险和时机，先解决最重要的问题，再逐步完善。<br>
In the past, I sometimes tried to make an engineering plan too complete at the start. My monorepo work taught me that a good tech idea may not be right for the team at that time. Now I first look at business value, change cost, risk, and timing. I solve the most important problem first, and then improve the plan step by step.

6. 你最有成就感的项目是什么？ / Which project are you most proud of?

我比较有成就感的是财经智能客诉 Agent。这个项目把原本滞后的用户客诉转化为线上风险信号。我主要负责文本分析、图片分析和 24 小时值班机器人。2026 年上半年，项目召回了 490+ 个线上问题，并拦截了 6 个资损问题。它的价值不只是用了 AI，而是让用户反馈更早地变成产研的行动。<br>
I am most proud of the finance customer complaint Agent. It turns late customer complaints into early warning signals. I mainly worked on text analysis, image analysis, and the 24-hour on-call bot. In the first half of 2026, the project found more than 490 online problems and stopped six problems that could cause money loss. Its main value is not only the use of AI. It helps the product and engineering teams take action earlier.

7. 当其他团队不同意你的方案时，你会怎么做？ / What do you do when another team does not agree with your plan?

我不会先试图证明对方错了，而是先了解他们的痛点和担忧。很多时候，一个方案对团队有收益，但风险和改造成本由业务项目承担，他们反对是合理的。我会根据收益、成本和风险调整方案，必要时先用小范围项目证明效果，再逐步推进。<br>
I do not start by trying to prove the other team is wrong. I first learn about their problems and worries. A plan may help the whole team, but the product team may have to take the cost and risk. Their concern can be fair. I change the plan based on value, cost, and risk. If needed, I test it with a small project first and then move forward step by step.

8. 请举一个没有成功的例子。 / Tell me about something that did not work.

我在大仓建设中尝试推动过主干开发，但最终没有完整落地。这件事让我意识到，方案在技术上成立，不等于团队已经准备好。因为团队没有相关的经验，基建也不足，且现有流程足够，强行推进反而会增加风险。现在我会更早判断团队是否具备条件，并先用小范围实践验证。<br>
In the monorepo project, I tried to move the team toward trunk-based development, but it was not fully adopted. This taught me that a plan can work in theory, but the team may not be ready for it. The team had little experience with this way of working, the supporting tools were not ready, and the current process worked well enough. Pushing it too fast would have added risk. Now I check whether the team is ready earlier and test the plan on a small scale first.

9. 同时有很多任务时，你如何排优先级？ / How do you set priorities when you have many tasks?

我会先看业务影响和风险。影响用户、资金、核心作业链路或大量开发者的问题优先处理。然后再看时间要求、依赖关系和投入产出比。我会先把目标、责任人和验收标准说清楚，再安排执行，避免只因为某个任务技术上更有趣就优先做。<br>
I first look at business impact and risk. Problems that affect users, money, key work, or many developers come first. Then I look at time, other work that depends on it, and the value for the cost. I make the goal, owner, and success check clear before the work starts. I do not choose a task first only because the tech is more interesting.

I want to keep growing in frontend engineering, complex business systems, and AI engineering. In the near term, I want to connect AI coding, Agent work, and product delivery more closely. In the long term, I want to own a wider technical area. I want to solve project problems and also turn good ideas into tools and ways of working that the whole team can use.
