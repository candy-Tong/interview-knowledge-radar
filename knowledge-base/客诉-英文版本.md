财经业务包括放心借、支付和保险等多个 C 端业务，每天产生约 5 万条客诉。传统客诉需要经过客服逐级上升到产品、研发和测试，链路长、上升速度慢；等产研感知到问题时，异常可能已经扩大为线上事故。<br>
The finance business has several customer products, such as loans, payments, and insurance. It receives about 50,000 customer complaints each day. In the traditional process, complaints move slowly from customer service to product, engineering, and QA teams. By the time these teams see the problem, it may have already grown into an online incident.

这个项目的目标是把客诉转化为线上监控信号，缩短用户反馈到产品、研发和测试的链路，更早识别监控和测试未覆盖的异常，在问题扩大前拦截线上事故。<br>
The goal of This project is to turn complaints into online warning signals, shorten the path from user feedback to us, and detect problems earlier. This allows us to catch online incidents before they grow.

我是这个项目的 Agent 开发负责人，主要建设了三条链路：文本分析链路负责场景识别、错误分类和聚量告警；图片分析链路用多模态模型识别白屏、按钮异常等前端问题；24 小时值班机器人负责查询完整客诉和知识，能够确认为非问题的自动降噪，无法确认的及时通知人工跟进。<br>
I was the Agent development owner. I mainly built three parts. The text flow finds the business case, finds the error type, and sends alerts when many users report the same problem. The image flow uses an AI model to find page problems such as blank pages and missing buttons. The 24-hour on-call bot checks the full complaint and saved knowledge. It removes known false alerts and sends unknown problems to people for follow-up.

2026 年上半年，高价值问题拦截率为 75%，并拦截了 6 个资损问题。6 月，值班机器人，人工跟进量下降约 80%。<br>
In the first half of 2026, It stopped 75% of high-value problems, including six problems that could cause money loss. In June, the on-call bot reduced manual follow-up by about 80%.
